I am always in awe of creative people, especially those who are visually creative, those who effortlessly put together a good color palette, and those who make it engaging for the viewer. My sense of aesthetic (when it shows up) is rather mundane (look at my attempt to visualise places on [a map in Vienna](/_posts/2025-07-28-vienna-guide.md)), but I'm trying to work on it by curating websites that I find appealing and fun (several of these were shared by my friend, Bianca). I would curate paintings and other art too, but their impact is reduced on screen, whereas websites are designed to be viewed on a screen!

* [_Fireflies_](https://ncase.me/fireflies/) by Nicky Case - An incredible interactive website to visualise the model describing how fireflies synchronise their flashing
* [_Absurd Trolley Problems_](https://neal.fun/absurd-trolley-problems/) and the [_Password Game_](https://neal.fun/password-game/) are a couple of really fun and interactive games designed by Neal Agarwal (you can find his other fun creations at https://neal.fun/)
* [_Complexity Explorables_](https://www.complexity-explorables.org/) by Dirk Brockmann - A collection of interactive explorable explanations of complex systems in biology, physics and methamtics, such as the Vicsek model, opinion dynamics and pulse-coupled oscillators.
* [_Of the Oak_](https://oftheoak.co.uk/oak-species) by Marshmallow Laser Feast - a digital, interactive journey created in collaboration by artists and scinetists celebrating oak trees as living monuments and networks connecting thousands of species that depend on them.
* [_The Collapse of Insects_](https://www.reuters.com/graphics/GLOBAL-ENVIRONMENT/INSECT-APOCALYPSE/egpbykdxjvq/): Illustrations by Catherine Tai - A great visualisation of the immense biodiversity of insects and the silent insect apocalypse
* [_Planets of Disparity_](https://vis.csh.ac.at/planets-of-disparity-two/): visualised by Liuhuaying Yang and Rainer Stütz - this website is an interactive game that visualises how spontaneous preferences can unintentionally create inequalities.
* [_Searching for Birds_](https://searchingforbirds.visualcinnamon.com/): designed by Nadieh Bremer - An interactive exploration of prominent birds in North America and how people search for them. [Visual Cinnamon](https://www.visualcinnamon.com/) has also created other delightful interactive visualisations
